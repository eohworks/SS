BoTICA

WORDMARK

The BoTICA wordmark is crafted from Ibarra Real type specimens. Sharpening serifs to align with the star from the flag of Puerto Rico. The top of the A leads in to to country code (PR).

TYPOGRAPHY

Originally a 18th-century Spanish typeface, Ibarra carries a historical resonance that subtly connects to Puerto Rico’s cultural past while still feeling refined and contemporary.

COLOR

Turquise evokes San Juan’s iconic facades, blending tradition with modernity.

BRAND ICON

Taking inspiration from Losas criollas, a traditional Puerto Rican cement tiles featuring colorful geometric patterns, reflecting the island’s colonial and artistic heritage.
